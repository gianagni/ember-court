Ember Court is an original duel card battler that plays offline in a desktop browser: you face the Cinder Magistrate across a candle-lit table, each side holding 20 Ember of life, and spend a growing pool of energy to play cards from four houses told apart by colour and shape — Flame (red triangle) burns, Ash (grey circle) shields, Coal (violet diamond) pierces shields, and Spark (gold star) draws cards and refunds energy. Cards combine: a second Flame in one turn deals +2 damage, Ash shields carry into the next turn, and Spark refunds keep long turns going, while the Magistrate announces its intent before every card so you can read and counter it. Click or press 1–5 to play, Enter to end your turn, and burn the Magistrate to zero before your own embers fade.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080
