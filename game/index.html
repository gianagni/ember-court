EMBER COURT

A duel of ember cards against the Cinder Magistrate. First to burn the other to 0 Ember wins.

Four houses, told by colour and shape: Flame ▲ red burns, Ash ● grey shields, Coal ◆ violet pierces shields, Spark ★ gold draws and refunds energy. Play a second Flame in one turn for +2 damage (Twin Flame). Ash shield carries to your next turn. Energy grows each turn. Click a card or press 1–5 to play it, Enter to end your turn.

Keyboard: 1–5 play a card · Enter ends the turn · mouse works too · sound is a tiny synth, no files
